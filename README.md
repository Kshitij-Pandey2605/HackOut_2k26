## 🚀 The Idea

**Carbon capture is only half the problem.**

Industries can capture CO₂, but after capture they still need to answer:

* Who can use this CO₂?
* Does the buyer need this level of purity?
* Can they consume the available quantity?
* Is the buyer close enough for practical transportation?
* Can the supplier be trusted?
* What happens when there is no direct buyer?

### CarbonSphere solves this gap.

CarbonSphere creates a digital marketplace and intelligence layer where **CO₂ suppliers can list captured carbon and buyers can discover compatible supply**.

Instead of simply showing listings, CarbonSphere evaluates potential matches based on:

**Purity + Quantity + Distance + Trust + Historical Success**

And when no direct buyer is found, the platform can suggest **potential alternative utilization pathways**.

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Today, captured CO₂ can become a stranded resource when suitable utilization opportunities are difficult to discover.

The challenge is not simply:

> **"Can we capture CO₂?"**

The next challenge is:

> **"Where can this captured CO₂ be used efficiently and economically?"**

A supplier may have available CO₂ but face:

```text
Limited buyer discovery
        ↓
Technical mismatch
        ↓
High transportation distance
        ↓
Unverified suppliers / buyers
        ↓
Unused or underutilized CO₂
```

CarbonSphere addresses this **post-capture utilization and matchmaking gap**.
