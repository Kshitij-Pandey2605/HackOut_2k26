## 🚨 The Problem

Carbon capture solves only the first half of the problem.

After CO₂ is captured, industries still need to answer:

> **Who can use it? Where can it be used? Is the quality suitable? What quantity is required? And is transporting it economically feasible?**

Today, this information is often fragmented across individual companies, suppliers, buyers and logistics providers.

This creates problems such as:

* Difficulty discovering suitable buyers
* Mismatch between CO₂ supply and demand
* Different purity and quantity requirements
* High transportation costs
* Lack of supplier trust and verification
* Limited visibility into alternative utilization opportunities

### The Gap

```text
CO₂ Captured
     ↓
      ❓
Who needs it?
Can they use this purity?
Can they consume this quantity?
Are they nearby?
Can we trust the supplier?
     ↓
CarbonSphere
```

---

# 💡 Our Solution

**CarbonSphere is a digital matchmaking platform that connects captured CO₂ suppliers with industries that can utilize CO₂ as a resource.**

Instead of simply listing suppliers and buyers, CarbonSphere evaluates their compatibility using:

* CO₂ purity
* Quantity
* Distance
* Supplier trust
* Historical transaction performance

When a direct buyer cannot be found, the platform can suggest **alternative CO₂ utilization pathways**.

### Our Core Idea

> **Don't just capture carbon. Find where it can create value.**

# 🚀 Key Innovation

## 1. 🤖 Smart Carbon Matching

CarbonSphere doesn't simply search for listings.

It **ranks compatible opportunities**.

### Compatibility Score

```text
30% → Purity Match
25% → Quantity Match
20% → Distance Match
15% → Supplier Trust
10% → Historical Success
```

### Formula

```text
Compatibility Score =
0.30(Purity)
+ 0.25(Quantity)
+ 0.20(Distance)
+ 0.15(Trust)
+ 0.10(Historical Success)
```

### Why this matters

A buyer doesn't just get:

> "Here are 50 suppliers."

They get:

> **"These are the suppliers most compatible with your requirements."**

### MVP Approach

The hackathon MVP uses an **explainable weighted scoring engine**.

It does not falsely claim to use a large trained ML model.

As transaction data grows, the scoring system can evolve into a machine-learning-based recommendation system.

```text

       ↓

       ↓

🦠 Algae Cultivation
🏗️ Concrete / Materials
```

### Why it is important

This changes CarbonSphere from:

**"A marketplace that finds buyers"**

into:

**"A platform that helps discover where captured carbon can create value."**

# 5. 🚚 Logistics-Aware Matching

CO₂ is a **physical resource**.

Therefore, finding a buyer isn't enough.

Transportation also matters.

CarbonSphere considers:

* Distance
* Estimated transportation cost
* Delivery time

Example:

```text
Supplier
Ahmedabad
    │
    │ 70 km
    ▼
Buyer
Vadodara
```

A nearby compatible buyer may be preferable to a distant buyer because logistics can strongly affect the economics of a physical CO₂ transaction.

# 🌍 Sustainability Impact

CarbonSphere aims to support a **circular carbon ecosystem**.

```text
Industrial CO₂
      ↓
Capture
      ↓
Match
      ↓
Transport
      ↓
Utilize
      ↓
Create Industrial Value
```

### Metrics We Can Track

* CO₂ supplied
* CO₂ matched
* CO₂ delivered
* CO₂ utilized
* Number of successful transactions
* Distance transported
* Utilization pathway

### Important

CarbonSphere does **not** assume:

> 1 tonne of CO₂ reused = 1 tonne of emissions avoided.

Actual climate impact depends on the utilization pathway, energy requirements, permanence and lifecycle emissions.

Therefore, impact figures should be treated as **potential/pathway-dependent estimates** unless independently verified.
